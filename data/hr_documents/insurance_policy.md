# Health Insurance Policy

## Eligibility

All full-time employees are eligible for the company's group health insurance plan after completing 30 days of employment.

## Coverage

The health insurance plan covers eligible employees and provides coverage for hospitalization and approved medical treatments according to the terms of the insurance provider.

## How to Claim Health Insurance

To submit a health insurance claim:

1. Inform the HR department about the hospitalization or medical treatment.
2. Collect all required medical bills, prescriptions, and hospital documents.
3. Submit the required documents through the company's insurance claim process.
4. The insurance provider will review the claim.
5. Approved claims will be processed according to the insurance policy terms.

## Claim Support

Employees who need help with a health insurance claim can contact the HR department.
