# Attendance Policy

Employees are expected to maintain regular attendance and report to work on time.

## Attendance

Employees must follow the company's official attendance procedures.

## Late Arrival

Repeated late arrivals may be reviewed by the HR department.

## Absence

Employees should inform their manager or the HR department when they are unable to attend work.

## Unauthorized Absence

Unauthorized or repeated absence may result in action according to company policy.
