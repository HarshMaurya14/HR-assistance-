# Remote Work Policy

This policy explains whether employees can work remotely, work from home (WFH), or perform remote work.

Remote work or working from home may be permitted depending on the employee's role and business requirements.

Employees must receive appropriate approval before working remotely or working from home when required.

Employees working remotely or from home are expected to remain available during their scheduled working hours.

Remote employees must follow company security and data protection policies.

## Key Information

- Can I work from home? Employees may be allowed to work from home depending on their role and business requirements.
- Is remote work allowed? Remote work may be permitted with appropriate approval.
- Can I do WFH? WFH (work from home) is covered under the company's remote work policy.
