# Working Hours Policy

## Standard Working Hours

Employees are expected to follow the company's official working schedule.

The standard working hours are from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday.

## Weekly Off

Saturday and Sunday are weekly holidays.

## Breaks

Employees are provided with appropriate breaks during the working day according to company policy.

## Flexible Working

Flexible working arrangements may be available depending on the employee's role and business requirements.
