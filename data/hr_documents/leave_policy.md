# Leave Policy

## Casual Leave

Employees are entitled to 12 casual leaves per calendar year.

Casual leave may be taken for personal work, emergencies, or short-term requirements.

Employees should apply for casual leave at least 1 day in advance whenever possible.

## Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to 10 sick leaves per calendar year.

Employees should inform their manager as soon as possible when taking sick leave.

## Annual Leave

Employees are entitled to 18 days of annual leave per calendar year.

Annual leave should normally be requested at least 7 days in advance.
