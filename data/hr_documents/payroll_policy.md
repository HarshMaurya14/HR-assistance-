# Payroll Policy

Employee salary payments are processed according to the company's payroll schedule.

Employees should contact the HR or payroll department regarding salary-related questions.

## Payslips

Employees may access their payslips according to the company's payroll procedures.

## Salary Deductions

Applicable deductions may be made according to company policies and legal requirements.

## Reimbursements

Employees should follow the company's reimbursement procedures when submitting eligible business expenses.
