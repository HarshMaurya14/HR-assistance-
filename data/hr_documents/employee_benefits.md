# Employee Benefits

The company provides eligible employees with benefits according to company policies.

## Health Insurance

Eligible employees may receive health insurance coverage according to the applicable insurance policy.

## Leave Benefits

Employees may receive Casual Leave, Sick Leave, and Annual Leave according to their eligibility and company policy.

## Additional Benefits

Additional employee benefits may be provided depending on company policy and employee eligibility.

Employees should contact the HR department for clarification regarding specific benefits.
